      Export

      Export writes every row and column to a file on this device. Nothing is sent anywhere.

      Wipe permanently deletes every observation Keel has recorded — every video, position and count — and shrinks the database file on disk. It cannot be undone, and it does not change anything YouTube knows about you. Your settings and blocklist are kept.

      What leaves this device

      Your raw recording stays here. At every currently available setting, the rows Keel keeps — what you were shown, when, and in what position — are never sent as rows or an ordered trail.

      Three network disclosures exist at every setting, including the default. Keel requests broad shared buckets, announces livestreams it sees, and exchanges a whole fixed-shape word-popularity aggregate that includes its local corpus. The live payload has no application-level sender, and the word pack has no plaintext words, video ids, edges or query. Direct peers still see ordinary connection metadata; see the Privacy policy for the full residual risk.

      At Broad sharing, Keel also answers requests with complete buckets of thousands of videos. Each bucket contains both claims cached from peers and aggregated, stringless recommendation claims derived from what you were shown; the response does not label which is which. A local claim contains counts grouped by rough position and day, not a raw timestamp, title, query, page-load row or ordered watch trail. Each neighbourhood uses an unlinkable claim identity, while the peer receiving it still sees your address, timing and the whole bucket. Level 3's STAR-protected cohort measurements and Level 4's attributed publication are not built and cannot be selected.
