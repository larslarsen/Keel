      Export

      Export writes every row and column to a file on this device. Nothing is sent anywhere.

      Wipe permanently deletes every observation Keel has recorded — every video, position and count — and shrinks the database file on disk. It cannot be undone, and it does not change anything YouTube knows about you. Your settings and blocklist are kept.

      What leaves this device

      Your recordings stay here. The rows Keel keeps — what you were shown, when, and in what position — are never sent anywhere, at any setting.

      One thing does leave, at every setting including the default: when Keel sees a livestream, it announces to other Keel users that the stream exists. That notice is a video id and title with no sender attached — it says a stream is on, not who saw it. It is what makes the Live tab work for everyone.

      Above the default, Keel also stores and passes on data other people published, and asks the network for recommendations in buckets of thousands of videos at once — never one video at a time — so a peer answering you cannot tell which one you wanted. Nothing you recorded is included. At v0.1.0 there is no published graph to fetch yet, so this stays dormant until a seed or bundle is imported. Publishing your own counts needs threshold encryption — your report stays sealed unless enough others report the same thing — and that is not built, so those settings send nothing of yours today.
