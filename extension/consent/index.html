Keel

Keel records which videos YouTube recommends to you, so you can see and control your own recommendations. Everything it records stays on this device.

What it records

The videos YouTube offers you on watch pages and the homepage — their title, channel, length, view count, and which position they appeared in.

What it does not record

Not what you watch. Not what you search for. Not who you are — there is no account and no sign-in. Nothing outside youtube.com.

Where it goes

Into a file on this computer, written by the Keel desktop app you install yourself. It is not sent to us — there is no server to send it to. You can export everything as JSON, or delete all of it, at any time.

Nothing is recorded until you press that. Recording is Keel — its suggestions, search and analysis are all computed from what it has kept here — so until you do, it has nothing to show you. If you change your mind later, remove the extension or delete the desktop app; the Your data page also erases everything it holds, permanently.

Sharing anything is separate, and off. Nothing leaves this computer unless you choose a contribution level on the Your data page, and the default is that nothing does.
