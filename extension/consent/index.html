Keel

Keel records which videos YouTube recommends to you, so you can see and control your own recommendations. It records only to this device, and only once you say so.

What it records

The videos YouTube offers you on watch pages and the homepage — their title, channel, length, view count, and which position they appeared in.

What it does not record

Not what you watch. Not what you search for. Not who you are — there is no account and no sign-in. Nothing outside youtube.com.

Where it goes

Into a file on this computer, written by the Keel desktop app you install yourself. It is not sent to us — there is no server to send it to. You can export everything as JSON, or delete all of it, at any time.

Recording is Keel. Its suggestions, search and analysis are all computed from what it has recorded on this device, so if you decline it will have nothing to show you — hiding YouTube's own suggestions still works, but the rest will be empty until you turn recording on. You can do that any time on the Your data page.

Sharing anything with anyone is a separate decision, off by default, and not built yet. Recording never sends anything anywhere.
